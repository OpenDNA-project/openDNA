# Names and logo

The code of openDNA is free software under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE): anyone may use it, study it, change it and share it,
including a modified version (a fork), under the same licence and with its source. This page is about something the licence
does not cover: the **names and the logo**.

**"openDNA"**, **"oDNA"** (open Design, Navigation & Assembly) and the oDNA wordmark and icon (in `docs/images/` and
`packaging/`) name the official project by Antoine Champie, published at
[github.com/OpenDNA-project/openDNA](https://github.com/OpenDNA-project/openDNA). They tell people which copy they are getting, and
who answers for it. They are not registered trademarks (yet); this page is how the project asks them to be used.

## What you can do without asking

- **Talk about the project** by its name: in a paper, a course, a review, a tutorial, a comparison.
- **Share unmodified copies** of an official release (the exe or the folder zip, as published) under their own name.
- **Say what your work is built on**: "based on openDNA", "a fork of openDNA", "compatible with openDNA
  project files (.odp)", written in a way that does not suggest it is the official project or endorsed by it.
- **Make a fork**, under the AGPL-3.0 like the original, with a name and an icon of its own (see below).

## What needs another name

A **modified version you share** (a fork, a rebuilt exe with changes, a hosted copy) must:

- use **a different name and icon**, one that cannot be taken for openDNA or oDNA (for example, not "openDNA Pro",
  "oDNA+" or "openDNA by …");
- change what the program shows as its own name: the window title, Help > About, the Welcome page, the update check and the
  "official download" address, which must not send its users to this project for support or updates;
- keep the copyright and licence notices (the AGPL asks for that anyway), and say that it is a modified version.

Selling a copy, paid support, or a service built on the code are allowed by the licence; using the names or the logo to
present it as the official project is not.

## Why

Forks are welcome: that is what the licence is for, and improvements can come back. The name only makes sure that someone
who downloads "openDNA" gets the real one, and that a bug reported here is a bug in this project's code.

Questions, or a use that is not covered here: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/OpenDNA-project/openDNA/issues).
